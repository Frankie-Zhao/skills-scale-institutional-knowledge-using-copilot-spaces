# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the target solution design, technical standards, and architecture decisions that help balanced delivery across the product lifecycle. They partner with engineering, PMs, and Product to ensure the solution remains scalable, maintainable, and aligned to business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns, integration approaches, and technical standards
- Review major design decisions and trade-offs with engineering leads
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and long-term risks
- Partner with Product and PMs to balance speed, quality, and maintainability
- Support roadmap planning with architecture roadmaps and sequencing guidance

### Goals
- Enable a robust, scalable, and secure technical foundation
- Reduce rework caused by avoidable architecture drift
- Align engineering decisions with delivery priorities and future growth

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical dependency planning with PM and engineering leads
- Cross-team decision records and trade-off summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: translates strategy and customer needs into feasible technical direction and trade-offs
- With Project Managers: helps sequence work, identify risks, and align delivery milestones with architecture dependencies
- With Developers: provides standards, design guidance, and review feedback to improve implementation quality
- With Stakeholders: explains technical trade-offs and the impact of decisions on timeline, cost, and operational risk

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure the solution meets organizational policies, security standards, and risk requirements throughout planning, development, and release. They help teams address security concerns early, reduce exposure, and coordinate response to emerging risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review product, infrastructure, and process design for security and compliance gaps
- Define security requirements, controls, and review checkpoints
- Coordinate with engineering and PMs on remediation planning and risk ownership
- Support incident response readiness and regulatory or policy alignment
- Educate teams on secure development practices and required approvals

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Promote secure-by-default design and delivery practices
- Enable safe, policy-aligned delivery without unnecessary friction

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Policy or compliance updates for project owners
- Escalation communication during security events or control gaps

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: ensures customer and business goals are balanced with compliance, privacy, and security constraints
- With Project Managers: helps identify dependency risks, review timelines, and coordinate governance checkpoints
- With Developers: reviews technical implementation against standards and supports remediation guidance
- With Stakeholders: communicates risk posture, control improvements, and approval needs where relevant

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Manager

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement Managers connect delivery work to customer outcomes, readiness, and adoption. They translate customer feedback, support issues, and rollout concerns into actionable delivery priorities and communication plans.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, usage patterns, and adoption blockers
- Partner with Product and PMs on rollout readiness, release communications, and support planning
- Identify customer-facing risks and help prioritize fixes or enablement work
- Coordinate change communication and adoption guidance for stakeholders
- Translate operational insights into backlog themes or improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption, satisfaction, and retention
- Reduce friction during rollout and post-launch support
- Align delivery work with real user needs and change readiness

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and enablement planning
- Release readiness and rollout briefings
- Cross-functional updates on adoption, support, and known issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: bring field-informed customer insights to prioritization and roadmap discussions
- With Project Managers: support communications, dependencies, and readiness planning around launches or adoption milestones
- With Developers: surface usability and reliability issues linked to customer experience and support workflows
- With Stakeholders: provide status on customer impact, readiness, and opportunities for improvement

---

## Business Analyst / Delivery Analyst

### Role Summary
Business and Delivery Analysts clarify requirements, improve process consistency, and support evidence-based decision-making across project delivery. They help connect business context, operational detail, and measurement to day-to-day execution.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify requirements, workflows, and success criteria across stakeholders
- Document process gaps, dependencies, and improvement opportunities
- Support reporting, metrics, and milestone tracking for delivery health
- Facilitate requirement translation between business and technical teams
- Help maintain decision logs, issue summaries, and process standardization

### Goals
- Improve clarity and consistency in delivery decisions and requirements
- Support measurable execution and informed prioritization
- Reduce ambiguity across teams and stakeholder groups

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and process reviews
- Status reporting and trend analysis for PM and leadership stakeholders
- Cross-functional documentation updates and decision tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: help refine acceptance criteria, metrics, and user-value framing
- With Project Managers: support planning quality, milestone tracking, and reporting accuracy
- With Developers: translate business needs into implementation-ready detail and validate assumptions
- With Stakeholders: align priorities, explain requirements trade-offs, and support governance decisions
